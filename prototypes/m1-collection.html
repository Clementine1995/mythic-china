Why these stories sit together

A journey, not one fixed bureaucracy.

The Collection follows how different materials imagine passage, judgment, return, haunting, and protection. It does not flatten every period, region, literary work, religious tradition, or local practice into one universal chart.

See a museum journey used to frame the scope

Editorial boundary

  • Layer traditions. Identify the text, object, period, region, or community behind a claim.
  • Keep roles precise. Zhong Kui is featured as a demon-quelling figure, not a Ten King or ruler of the whole realm.
  • Separate reception. Modern adaptations explain attention today; they do not prove older facts.
