3 × 2 Collection family board

One brand, six spatial grammars.

The Chinese Underworld is confirmed. The other five are lightweight visual probes only: no stable IDs, routes, production assets, or publication commitments.

Confirmed Collection

The Chinese Underworld

Threshold, low horizon, downward route, deep occlusion. Stone mist and damp mineral surfaces.

Motion: one dark reveal with at most 2% downward movement.

Visual probe only

Defying Heaven

Rising diagonal, oversized negative space, and scale contrast. Storm air and wind-cut rock.

Motion probe: slight lift and cloud fade.

Visual probe only

Strange Tales After Dark

Off-axis framing, partial occlusion, and an implied presence beyond the frame. Smoke green and night mist.

Motion probe: local crossfade, never glitch.

Visual probe only

Shan Hai Jing Field Guide

Specimen plate, map logic, scale markers, and topographic layers. Metadata leads the composition.

Motion probe: annotations appear in sequence.

Visual probe only

Festival Myths

Circular gathering, clustered objects, and reflected surfaces. Warm lacquer and textile light.

Motion probe: a short rhythmic reveal, no looping fireworks.

Visual probe only

Mortals & Legends

Eye-level figure plane, stable horizon, and equal weight for person and setting. Cloth, wood, and stone.

Motion probe: near-static figure reveal.

Subject-specific reference board

The source decides the claim, not the atmosphere.

Black Myth appears only as modern adaptation context. None of its characters, logos, screenshots, typography, palette, framing, or code enters the prototype.

Collection scope

Asian Art Museum: Hell, Arts of Asian Underworlds

Supports journey-based framing and the coexistence of serious, strange, and humorous materials. It does not prove one universal Chinese system.

System boundary

Columbia: Ten Magistrates of the Underworld

Supports a teaching entry into judgment and rebirth. Used to distinguish Zhong Kui from the Ten Magistrates, not to generalize every tradition.

Zhong Kui identity

The Met: Zhong Kui, the Demon Queller with Five Bats

Supports the English “Demon Queller” identity. It does not independently settle origins, fixed offices, or all later versions.

Modern adaptation only

Game Science: Black Myth: Zhong Kui

Supports current adaptation context and reader attention only. It is never evidence for ancient, religious, literary, or folk claims.
